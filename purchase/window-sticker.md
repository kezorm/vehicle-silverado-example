# Original Window Sticker — Transcription

> **⚠️ FABRICATED.** Illustrative of format and practice, not a real build.

Source: Monroney label (federally mandated new-vehicle window sticker).
In a real record the PDF sits here as `window-sticker.pdf` with
`window-sticker.txt` beside it.

---

## Why transcribe a ten-year-old window sticker

**It is the only complete statement of how the vehicle left the factory.**

By the time a truck reaches a third owner, options have been added, removed,
broken, and replaced. The window sticker is the datum against which all of that
is measured. It answers questions no later document can:

- Was the trailering package factory or added later?
- Did it come with the locking differential, or was that a previous owner's swap?
- Which of the current tires, wheels and accessories are original?

None of that is actionable today. All of it becomes actionable the moment
something breaks, or the truck is sold and a buyer asks.

**GM makes original window stickers retrievable by VIN for many model years.**
If the original is lost, it is often recoverable — worth trying before
concluding the information is gone.

## What this settles that nothing else could

> **The locking rear differential (`G80`) and the 3.42 axle (`GT4`) are
> factory.** A parts counter asking "which rear axle?" now has a documented
> answer that does not depend on anyone crawling under the truck, and a future
> buyer has evidence the differential is not an aftermarket swap.

Cross-check against the RPO label — the sticker says what was *ordered*, the RPO
label says what was *built*. They almost always agree; when they do not, the RPO
label wins, and the disagreement is itself worth recording.
